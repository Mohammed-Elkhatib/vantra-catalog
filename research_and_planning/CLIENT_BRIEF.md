### Project Overview: Vantra Website Development
 * Business Context: Vantra is a new "sister company" based in Lebanon, specializing in medical-grade air filtration systems, dampers, and related hospital infrastructure, here is it's link: https://www.cmsglobal.com
 * Company Structure: Vantra operates as a subsidiary of a parent company in the UAE, here is the link: https://ventra-leb.com
 * Project Status: The domain and basic setup have been initiated, and the project is now ready for further development.
 * Timeline: The client is not under immediate pressure and prefers a steady, well-executed workflow.

### Core Objectives & Requirements
 * Website Functionality:
   * Accessibility: The client wants to remove the barrier of requiring visitors to provide personal contact details before accessing the product catalog.
   * Direct Access: The catalog should be open to all users, mimicking a clean, user-friendly, and open browsing experience rather than a gated one.
   * Design & Content: The client aims for a design that matches the professional standard of the parent company (CMS Global) while exploring improvements in design and user interface.
   * AI Integration: The client is interested in possibility of incorporating AI into the platform to enhance features and improve the user experience.
 * Organization:
   * The platform needs to categorize products effectively, specifically targeting filtration types (Pre-filter, Carbon, Fine, and HEPA filters).
   * The client intends to provide a constant stream of catalog data that can be uploaded or managed through the website backend.
